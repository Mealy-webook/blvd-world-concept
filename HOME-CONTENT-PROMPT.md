# BLVD World — home page content, as a prompt

Everything below is the live copy and data of the home page at
`https://mealy-webook.github.io/blvd-world-concept/`, in the order it appears.
Hand this to a designer, a copywriter, or a model and it should be enough to rebuild the
page without seeing it.

---

## Brief

Build the landing page for **BLVD World** — a theme park in Boulevard City, Riyadh, part
of **Riyadh Season**, where each zone recreates a different country. Every ticket,
package and table is booked on **webook.com**; the page's job is to make someone want to
go and then hand them off to a booking.

**Tone:** confident, plain, specific. Short sentences. Concrete nouns over adjectives —
"kabsa on the grill" rather than "authentic local cuisine". Nothing exclamatory.
**Setting:** it is a night park. It opens in the late afternoon and runs past midnight.
**Currency:** Saudi Riyal, written `SAR` (the globe-hero variant uses the ﷼ glyph).

## 10 — Good to know

Title:

> **GOOD TO KNOW**

Five questions, as an accordion:

**Where is BLVD World?**
BLVD World sits in Riyadh's Boulevard City district, part of Riyadh Season. Parking and
ride-hailing drop-offs are signposted from the main gates.

**What does the entry ticket include?**
Entry gives you access to BLVD World and every walkable zone — the streets, plazas, souks
and light shows. Individual rides, dining and ticketed experiences are booked separately.

**What are the opening hours?**
BLVD World opens in the late afternoon and runs past midnight through the Season. Exact
times shift by day, so check your ticket for the night you're visiting.

**Is it suitable for children?**
Yes — most zones are family areas, with carousels, arcades and dining alongside the
bigger rides. Individual thrill rides carry their own height and age limits.

**Can I change or refund a booking?**
Changes and refunds follow the policy shown at checkout on webook.com, which varies by
ticket type. Check the terms on your confirmation email.

## Things that are not known, and must not be filled in

- **Ride heights, age limits and maintenance status** — no data for any ride.
- **Which zone a priced ride is in** — the fourteen priced rides and the zones' own ride
  lists are disjoint sets; no name appears in both.
- **Shops and retail** — no data at all.
- **Social media handles** — none anywhere in the project.
- **Guest reviews or testimonials** — none. Do not write any.
- **Photography for five of the ten experiences** — Swimming with Dolphins, Battle Dome,
  the Lion Experience, Pac-Man and PUBG have no picture.
