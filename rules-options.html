Venue rules — four options

All four use the nine Figma icons and the five real rules, on the site's own tokens. What changes is the structure. Today's section stacks a nine-icon row on top of a numbered list, which shows everything at once and asks the reader to work out which parts matter to them.

01

Can / cannot

The one option that answers the question people actually arrive with. The nine icons already split cleanly into three things the venue offers and six it forbids — this stops hiding that split and leads with it. My pick.

The back of the ticket

Each rule as a stub torn off a ticket, in one sideways strip. Keeps the section short whatever the rule count, and the ticket metaphor is already the campaign's. Costs you the at-a-glance overview.

Icons beside an accordion

Closest to what is there now, and the least disruptive: the nine icons become a 3×3 block instead of a nine-wide row that squeezes on a phone, and the five rules collapse so the section stops being a wall of legal text. Shortest section of the four.

03Cameras

No professional cameras and video recorders are permitted at the venue.

04Photography of guests

The ticket holder consents to photographs, video and audio recordings of the ticket holder being captured and used in printed and electronic media, including the internet, as an attendee at the venue.

05Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited on the premises, including e-cigarettes. The use of lighters and matches is also prohibited inside the site, except in areas designated for smoking.
