## Restaurant brand plates (not real logos)

The four restaurant cards on `#/eats` show a wordmark plate built from this
project's own type and a plain geometric mark — not the restaurants' logos. No
official logo for Tante, Superheroes, Sakura Lounge or EOS exists in the BLVD
World media library (it holds venue photographs and football-club marks only),
and the real marks are the brands' trademarks. Replace `brand` in `js/data.js`
with the supplied artwork when it arrives.

The four food photographs remain in `img/food/` and are still used by the map's
dining rows; their licences are recorded above.

## Hero footage

`video/hero.mp4` (1280x720, 10s, 24fps, 2.4 MB, no audio) is the BLVD World
landmark panorama from the Figma board, node 16043:3589 — Chichen Itza, the Taj
Mahal, Grendizer, the Eiffel Tower, the Kuwait Towers, the Moroccan gate, Giza,
the pagoda, the lake with its gondola and dolphins, and fireworks. Generated from
a still and supplied by the user. It plays muted, inline and looped, and holds on
its first frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Figma cannot supply this file: `export_video` renders a frame's *timeline*, and a
video *fill* is not a timeline, so it returns a 2s still.

### What was patched out, and how

The delivered clip had three defects. All three were removed with ffmpeg by
copying clean neighbouring pixels over them — a feathered `overlay` of a crop
taken from the same frame, so the patch animates with the footage instead of
freezing. `delogo` was tried first and rejected: over rippling water it leaves a
flat interpolated plate, and where a box met the stone kerb it dragged kerb
pixels down into the lake.

| Defect | Source box | Patched from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generator sparkle in the water | x1124 y563 72x72 | x1190, same rows |
| Garbled Arabic Riyadh Season mark | x1116 y0 84x46 | x1032, same rows |
| "RITAON SEASON" — misspelt lockup | x1210 y0 70x46 | x1046, same rows |

The tower's antenna sits at x1203..1206 between the two sky marks and is
deliberately left untouched — the two patches stop either side of it.

Verified on all 240 frames: the two sky boxes go from a peak luminance of 255 to
93 (sky and one star), the antenna column holds at 111 before and after, and the
sparkle's own footprint drops from 381-514 bright pixels to the level of the
surrounding water. Outside the three boxes the frame differs only by x264
re-encode noise. Re-encoded once, at crf 23.

ffmpeg came from `pip install --user imageio-ffmpeg`; it is not otherwise on this
machine.

## Restaurant prices are placeholders

`from` on each of the four restaurants in `js/data.js` (SAR 120-220) is invented.
The earlier version carried the real booking condition instead — "Reservations on
webook", "Book ahead, queues after 7 PM" — because no price for any of these
kitchens exists in what we have. Those lines were replaced with start prices on
request. Replace the numbers before this goes anywhere public.
